# Instox — The Pitch
## Why Small Shop Owners Need This

---

## The Problem

**73% of small retailers in India manage inventory with pen, paper, and calculator.**

I know this because I am one of them.

My family runs a small textile and footwear shop in Kerala. Every time new stock arrives, we spend 2-3 hours:
- Matching physical bills with products
- Calculating 30% markup on each item by hand
- Writing prices on stickers
- Putting items on shelves

And the worst part? **We have no idea if we're actually profitable.**

We don't know:
- How much profit we made on each sale
- Which products are actually selling
- When we're running low on stock
- How much money is stuck in unsold inventory

**We just go with the flow, assuming we're making money.**

Many small businesses fail because they don't know their actual margins.

---

## The Solution

**Instox** — a simple billing and inventory app for small shop owners.

**Core Features:**
1. **Auto Price Calculator** — enter wholesale price, system adds 30% (or your custom margin)
2. **Profit Tracking** — every sale shows exactly how much profit you made
3. **Product Search** — find any product in 2 seconds when customer asks
4. **Smart Billing** — generate bills where customer sees ONLY retail price (profit hidden)
5. **Low-Stock Alerts** — never run out of popular items
6. **Photo Capture** — take picture of product instead of typing everything
7. **Sales Analytics** — see daily/monthly profit reports with charts

## How I Built It (Product Thinking)

1. **Problem Discovery:** I am the user. I experience the pain every day.
2. **User Research:** Interviewed 5 other shop owners. Same problems.
3. **PRD:** Wrote product requirements with success metrics.
4. **User Stories:** 13 stories with acceptance criteria.
5. **Prioritization:** MoSCoW method — Must/Should/Could/Won't.
