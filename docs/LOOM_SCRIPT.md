# Loom Video Script — Instox
## 2-Minute Pitch for Zamp

---

### Opening (0:00 - 0:15)

**[Show yourself on camera]**

"Hi, I'm Nivedya. I'm applying for the APM role at Zamp.

I built Instox — a billing and inventory app for small shop owners.

Let me show you why it matters and where it goes."

---

### Problem (0:15 - 0:45)

**[Switch to screen share — show your shop or a small shop]**

"My family runs a small textile shop in Kerala.

Every time new stock arrives, we spend 2-3 hours:
- Manually calculating 30% markup on each item
- Writing prices on stickers
- Matching physical bills with products

And the worst part? We have no idea if we're actually profitable.

We don't know how much money we make on each sale, which products are selling, or when we're running low on stock.

63 million small shops in India face this exact problem."

---

### Solution (0:45 - 1:15)

**[Show the app — demo the core features]**

"Instox solves this with 5 core features.

First, auto price calculator — enter wholesale price, system adds your margin.

Second, profit tracking — every sale shows exactly how much profit you made.

Third, product search — find any product in 2 seconds when customer asks.

Fourth, smart billing — generate bills where customer sees only retail price, profit stays hidden.

Fifth, low-stock alerts — never run out of popular items."

---

### How I Built It (1:15 - 1:35)

**[Show GitHub repo — PRD, user stories]**

"I built this in 2 weeks, from problem discovery to deployment.

But I didn't just code. I did product work first.

I interviewed 5 shop owners. Wrote a PRD with 13 user stories. Prioritized features using MoSCoW.

Then I built it — Flask backend, SQLite database, responsive frontend.

3 versions in 2 weeks."

---

### Vision (1:35 - 1:50)

**[Show roadmap — V4, V5, V6]**

"Instox starts as a billing app. But the vision is bigger.

V4 adds intelligence — demand forecasting, smart reordering.

V5 becomes a platform — multi-store support, WhatsApp integration.

V6 becomes an ecosystem — marketplace, financing, insurance.

Instox becomes the operating system for small retail in India."

---

### Close (1:50 - 2:00)

**[Back to camera]**

"I'm looking for an APM role where I can build products that solve real problems.

Instox is proof that I can identify problems, define solutions, and ship working products.

Thank you for watching. I'd love to talk."

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Keep it under 2 minutes** — practice first
2. **Show your face at start and end** — builds connection
3. **Screen share for demo** — show the actual app
4. **Speak naturally** — don't read script word-for-word
5. **Record in quiet place** — no background noise
6. **Use Loom** — free, easy to record and share
